🎞️ What Is Animation?

Animation is the process of creating the illusion of movement by showing images or drawings in sequence.

🎨 Parts of Animation

Part Meaning
Character The person, animal, or object being animated.
Movement The action or motion shown in the animation.
Background The environment where the scene happens.
Sound Music, voices, and effects that support the scene.

💡 Why Animation Interests Me

Animation combines drawing, storytelling, movement, technology, and imagination. That is why I find it interesting to learn about.
